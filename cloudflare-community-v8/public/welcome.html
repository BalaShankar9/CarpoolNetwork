Three steps to travel together

  1. 1

    Make your profile

    Choose Sign in / Join on a computer or Account on your phone. Create an account, verify your email code, add your town and WhatsApp number, and submit a clear profile photo for moderator approval.

    Drivers also add their registration and passenger seats in Account → Vehicle & social profiles.

  2. 2

    Find a ride or offer your seats

    Choose your towns, date and time. Type a town, choose it on the map or optionally use your current location. Drivers can search for nearby passenger requests and adjust the pickup radius.

  3. 3

    Confirm, then connect

    A seat is confirmed when the driver accepts. Use My bookings to check its status and open your booking chat. Accepted partners can also connect on WhatsApp. Agree pickup, timing and any contribution together.

KEEP IN TOUCH

Chat in one place

Use community conversations and private booking chats. Online presence shows recent connection activity.

GOING REGULARLY?

Plan your commute

In My bookings → Regular commutes, drivers can invite connected ride partners to a private group. Choose up to four weeks of dates. Request and accept seats for each date separately.

ON THE DAY

Share your progress

The driver starts the confirmed trip from its booking. Participants can choose to share their live location. Sharing stops when the trip screen is closed or hidden, or you press Stop. The driver finishes the trip.

BRING YOUR COMMUNITY

Pass it on.

Share the guide or image with people who travel your way. Both include the website and a QR code.

A little help when you need it

If plans change, cancel promptly in My bookings. Use the member reporting controls for a concern about someone, or Report a problem for a website issue.

Profile approval and email verification do not establish identity. Vehicle records do not establish insurance or current roadworthiness. Read Travelling safely before your first trip.
