A little help when you need it

Start with our guide for joining, finding a ride and confirming a seat.

Account or booking help

Sign in and open Help & Support to start a private support conversation. If you cannot sign in, email balashankarbollineni4@gmail.com.

A booking request is confirmed only when the driver accepts it. Check My bookings and agree pickup, timing and any contribution with your confirmed partner before travelling.

Feedback and website problems

Use Leave feedback to suggest an improvement. Use Report a bug if something does not work. You can send either without signing in. Reports go to a private review inbox; keep the reference shown after a successful submission.

Safety or behaviour concerns

Use the report control on the member, post or conversation concerned, or contact Support. Include only information needed to explain the concern. Reports are not monitored as an emergency service; if there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Your information

Account security includes export and deletion controls. For another privacy request, or if you cannot access your account, email the contact above. We may need to confirm account ownership before acting. Do not send passwords, email codes or recovery codes.
