Before you travel

Carpool Network helps members find and request shared rides. Members arrange the actual journey with each other. A profile or accepted booking is not proof of a person's identity, driving licence, insurance or vehicle condition. When enabled, an SMS code checks access to a number; it does not verify a person’s identity or WhatsApp account.

  • Confirm who you are travelling with and agree the pickup, destination, timing and any contribution before setting off.
  • Choose a public pickup location. Share your trip plans with someone you trust.
  • Drivers should check that their licence, vehicle and insurance permit the journey. Riders should ask about seatbelts and any other needs before accepting a ride.
  • Do not send money or sensitive documents because of pressure from another member. Keep account recovery codes private.
  • If plans change, cancel promptly in My bookings so seats and requests update.
  • Use the member or post reporting controls for concerning behaviour, and Help & Support for a private support conversation.

If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Website reports are not an emergency response channel.
