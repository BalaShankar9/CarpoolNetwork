Your information

Carpool Network helps members arrange shared journeys. This preview is for testing, with a separate database from the existing service.

Accounts and verification

Your name, town and the profile details you choose to display help members find each other. Email addresses are used privately for sign-in and verification. A verified email means that an inbox was accessible; it does not verify identity, a phone number, a driving licence or insurance. Cloudflare delivers sign-in emails. Codes expire after ten minutes and are single-use. Expired challenges are removed after one day during daily maintenance.

A secure session cookie remembers sign-in. Browser storage keeps preferences and unsent message drafts on your device. Account security lets you end other sessions. Keep recovery codes private.

What other members can see

Listings, public profiles, comments and ratings may be visible to visitors. The community lounge is visible to signed-in members. Community and private conversation access is controlled by membership. A booking conversation is shared with its participants. Avoid placing exact addresses or private contact details in public content. A member must supply a WhatsApp contact and agree to share it before posting, booking or sending messages. It is visible only to their accepted or completed ride partners, including in connected profiles and booking conversations. Pending, declined, cancelled and unrelated bookings do not reveal it. Numbers are self-provided, not verified by WhatsApp or used as proof of identity. Changing this contact does not change your sign-in credentials. Blocking a member hides the contact in this app, but cannot erase a number that someone has already saved.

Online counts reflect recently active visible connections in that conversation. They do not guarantee a member is available. Chat is protected in transit and access-controlled; it is not end-to-end encrypted. Authorised operators can access stored data to operate the service and investigate reports.

Opening WhatsApp

The Open WhatsApp link passes the selected partner’s number and a generic suggested greeting to WhatsApp. You choose whether to send the message there. Carpool Network does not read or store that WhatsApp conversation. WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy apply.

Photos, vehicle records and social links

Profile photographs are resized, stripped of image metadata and stored in Cloudflare R2. New photos require moderator review before publication. An approved photo is visible on your public profile and listings. Where supported, an on-device face detector can assist submission; it does not identify the person, compare faces or create an identity-verification badge. Replaced or rejected images become eligible for deletion after 30 days in daily maintenance. Deleted accounts lose public photo access immediately and stored photo objects enter the maintenance deletion queue.

A vehicle check sends your registration to the DVLA Vehicle Enquiry Service through our server. We store the returned make, colour, year, fuel, MOT and tax records with the check time and your declared passenger-seat count. A record check does not confirm vehicle ownership, insurance, a driving licence or current roadworthiness. Signed-in members see vehicle details with a masked registration; you and confirmed partners for current or future journeys can see the full registration. Optional Instagram and Facebook links are member-provided public profile links, not verified account connections.

Location choices

The current-town button requests device location once and finds a nearby UK town on your device. Exact GPS coordinates are not uploaded by that feature. Searching sends the town you choose and radius settings to this service. Distances are straight-line estimates between town centres, not driving routes. Town suggestions use GeoNames data under CC BY 4.0. Optional town maps load from OpenFreeMap only when you open the map. That provider receives your IP address and requests for the map areas you view; the current-town feature does not send it your precise GPS reading. Map data is from OpenStreetMap contributors. You can always type a town without opening a map. Live trip tracking is not enabled in this preview.

Messages and moderation

Messages remain after reconnecting or signing in on another device. Chat text is cleared after twelve months during maintenance. Removing a message clears its visible text; evidence already attached to a safety report may be retained for investigation. Evidence in resolved safety reports is cleared after 180 days. Account security includes account export and deletion controls. Booking and moderation records may be retained in anonymised form after account deletion.

Automatic technical reports

Failed requests and application errors can send a limited report to this app's Cloudflare-hosted service: error category, a general route, release, time, count and app file line numbers. Automatic reports do not copy form contents, message text, recovery codes, session tokens, screenshots or query strings. Cloudflare also processes connection information to serve and protect requests.

Reports you send

Report a problem works without signing in. Descriptions are private to authorised administrators; a signed-in report may link to your account. Do not include passwords, codes, payment details or exact private addresses. Keep the submission reference. Resolved or ignored technical reports are removed after 90 days without another occurrence; open reports remain for investigation.

Get help

Use Help & Support for account or privacy requests. If you cannot sign in, use Report a problem. Reports are not an emergency service and do not guarantee an immediate response.
