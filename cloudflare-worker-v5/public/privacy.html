Privacy and problem reports

Carpool Network uses your name, area and the profile details you choose to display to help members find each other. Your posts, comments and ratings can be viewed by other visitors. Avoid putting private contact or pickup details in public posts.

Accounts and contact details

Your phone number is used for your account and WhatsApp contact. Ride contact links become available to the people in an accepted booking. Keep your recovery code private: it can restore account access. The service uses a secure session cookie and browser storage to remember your sign-in and ride search.

Automatic technical reports

When the website detects a failed request or an application error, it sends a limited technical report to Carpool Network's own Cloudflare-hosted service. It includes the error category, a general page or API route, release version, time, occurrence count and, where available, app file line numbers. These reports do not copy form contents, recovery codes, session tokens, phone numbers, screenshots or URL query strings. Network providers still process normal connection information when serving requests.

Reports you send

The Report a problem button works without signing in. Your description is stored privately for authorised administrators to investigate. A signed-in report may be linked to your account. Please do not include passwords, recovery codes, payment details or exact private addresses. Keep the reference shown after submission. Sending a report does not guarantee an immediate response.

Resolved or ignored technical reports are removed after 90 days without another occurrence. Open reports are kept while they need investigation. Support conversations, booking records and moderation records are managed separately.

Get help

Use Help & Support while signed in for an account or privacy request. If you cannot sign in, use Report a problem and explain what help you need. Do not send your recovery code. Reports are not monitored as an emergency service.
