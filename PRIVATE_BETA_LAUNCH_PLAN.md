# PRIVATE BETA LAUNCH PLAN

**Date:** 2026-03-09
**Product:** CarpoolNetwork v1 — Private Beta
**Domain:** carpoolnetwork.co.uk
**Goal:** Launch to 1–3 existing WhatsApp ride-sharing communities within 4–6 weeks

---

## Who Launches First

### Ideal First Community Profile

- An existing WhatsApp group of 30–100 members
- Already actively sharing rides (at least a few rides per week)
- Language-based or location-based community (e.g., Telugu community in Birmingham, Polish community in London)
- Has a clear community organiser/admin who can champion the product
- Members are commuters with recurring routes OR event-goers (temple, cultural events)

### Launch Sequence

| Wave | When | Who | Size | Goal |
|------|------|-----|------|------|
| Wave 0 | Week 1 | Founder + 5 testers | 5–10 | Smoke test all flows |
| Wave 1 | Week 2–3 | First community (founder's own network) | 30–50 | Real usage, community flow validation |
| Wave 2 | Week 4–5 | Second community (different language/location) | 30–50 | Cross-community validation |
| Wave 3 | Week 6+ | Third community (organic referral or outreach) | 30–50 | Scaling test |

### How to Onboard First Communities

**Step 1: Founder identifies 1–2 WhatsApp groups they have access to**
- Ideally groups where founder is a member or knows the admin personally
- Ask the group admin: "Can I try something that might make our ride-sharing easier?"

**Step 2: Founder creates the community on CarpoolNetwork**
- Uses admin panel to create community
- Generates invite link/code
- Sets community name, type, language

**Step 3: Founder posts in WhatsApp group**
Message template:
> 🚗 Hey everyone! I've been building something to make our ride-sharing less chaotic.
> 
> Instead of scrolling through 200 messages to find a ride, you can now:
> ✅ Post rides with pickup time & route
> ✅ Search for rides by date and destination  
> ✅ Book a seat in one tap
> ✅ Message the driver directly
> 
> It's free, it's for our community only, and it takes 2 minutes to set up.
> 
> Join here: https://carpoolnetwork.co.uk/signup?community=INVITE_CODE
> 
> Try it for our next [event/commute/weekend trip]!

**Step 4: Founder personally onboards the first 10 users**
- Help them create accounts
- Post the first 3–5 rides manually
- Be the first driver AND the first passenger

**Step 5: Community organiser becomes community admin**
- Grant them moderator role
- Show them how to post announcements
- Make them co-owner of the community

## How to Position Product vs WhatsApp

### What to Say

> "CarpoolNetwork doesn't replace WhatsApp. It sits alongside it. 
> 
> WhatsApp is great for chatting. But when you want to find a ride for Tuesday to Birmingham, scrolling through 200 messages isn't great.
> 
> CarpoolNetwork is your community's ride board. Post rides, find rides, book seats — all in one place. Then coordinate details on WhatsApp or in our chat."

### What NOT to Say

- Don't say "better than WhatsApp" — people are loyal to WhatsApp
- Don't say "stop using WhatsApp" — that's a losing battle
- Don't say "download our app" — it's a website, works on any phone browser
- Don't frame it as a replacement — frame it as a complement

## What the First Admin Workflow Should Be

### Founder's Daily Beta Routine (15 min/day)

1. **Check admin dashboard** — New signups, active rides, recent bookings
2. **Check bug reports** — Any crashes or UX complaints?
3. **Check messages/community page** — Any inappropriate content?
4. **Post in WhatsApp group** — "Anyone heading to [X] this week? Post it on CarpoolNetwork!"
5. **DM one user** — "How was your experience? Anything confusing?"

### Admin Panel Focus (Beta)

Only use these 5 admin pages:
- `/admin` — Dashboard overview
- `/admin/users` — See who signed up, verify identities
- `/admin/rides` — Monitor ride activity
- `/admin/bugs` — Track reported issues
- `/admin/beta` — Manage allowlist

Ignore everything else (analytics, performance, bulk ops, etc.) until there are 200+ users.

## What Success Looks Like

### Week 1 (Wave 0): Internal Testing
- [ ] 5 users can sign up, complete onboarding, join community
- [ ] 3 rides posted, 2 bookings made
- [ ] Messaging works between driver and passenger
- [ ] No critical bugs or crashes
- [ ] Mobile web experience is smooth on iPhone + Android

### Week 2–3 (Wave 1): First Real Community
- [ ] 20+ users from one community signed up
- [ ] 10+ rides posted by real users (not founder)
- [ ] 5+ bookings completed
- [ ] At least 1 ride with review/rating submitted
- [ ] Community page has 3+ posts
- [ ] No user reports "I don't understand how to use this"

### Week 4–5 (Wave 2): Second Community
- [ ] Second community onboarded with different language/region
- [ ] Communities can see their own rides (not mixed)
- [ ] 50+ total users across both communities
- [ ] At least 1 ride posted without founder's involvement
- [ ] Retention: 30%+ of Wave 1 users return in Week 4

### Week 6+ (Wave 3): Scaling Signal
- [ ] Third community joins via word of mouth
- [ ] 100+ total users
- [ ] NPS score > 30 from beta survey
- [ ] At least 3 users say "this is better than WhatsApp for finding rides"
- [ ] Clear feedback on what to build next

## Beta Metrics

### Primary Metrics (Track Weekly)

| Metric | Target (Week 4) | Why It Matters |
|--------|-----------------|----------------|
| Registered users | 50+ | Adoption |
| Rides posted | 20+/week | Supply |
| Bookings made | 10+/week | Demand |
| Booking → Completion rate | 60%+ | Follow-through |
| Messages sent | 30+/week | Coordination happening |
| Return users (weekly) | 30%+ | Retention |

### Secondary Metrics (Track Monthly)

| Metric | Why |
|--------|-----|
| Community invite conversion | Are users inviting others? |
| Time to first ride post | How fast do new users create value? |
| Rides per driver per week | Are drivers returning? |
| Average rating | Is the trust model working? |
| Bug reports | Is the product stable? |

### Don't Track Yet

- Revenue (it's free)
- Cost per acquisition (it's manual outreach)
- Lifetime value (too early)
- Conversion funnels (not enough volume)

## How to Collect Feedback

### In-App
- Bug report button (already exists)
- Simple "How was your ride?" after ride completion (already exists via reviews)

### Manual
- WhatsApp message to each beta user after their first ride
- Weekly poll in community WhatsApp group: "What's one thing CarpoolNetwork should improve?"
- Monthly 1:1 calls with 3–5 most active users

### Survey (Week 4)
Simple Google Form:
1. How often do you share rides?
2. How did you find rides before CarpoolNetwork?
3. On a scale of 0–10, how likely are you to recommend CarpoolNetwork to another community?
4. What's the most useful feature?
5. What's the most confusing part?
6. What one thing would make you use it more?

## Founder Manual Ops Plan

### Things the Founder Does Manually in Beta

| Task | Frequency | Why |
|------|-----------|-----|
| Create communities in DB | Per community onboard | No self-serve community creation yet |
| Generate invite codes | Per community | Admin-created |
| Post first rides in new communities | First week per community | Seed supply |
| Respond to bug reports | Daily | Build trust |
| Message new users | Per signup | Onboarding support |
| Moderate community posts | Daily | No automated moderation needed yet |
| Share rides on WhatsApp | 2–3x/week | Bridge users to platform |
| Ban/remove bad actors | As needed | Manual moderation |
| Collect feedback | Weekly | Iteration signal |

### What the Founder Should NOT Do

- Do NOT obsess over admin dashboards and analytics
- Do NOT build new features mid-beta
- Do NOT try to scale to more than 3 communities
- Do NOT spend time on SEO, marketing pages, or social media
- Do NOT worry about Stripe/payments
- Do NOT try to make the app "feature-complete"

## Risks in First Rollout

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| **Community model not built in time** | Medium | 🔴 Blocks launch | Start building NOW; scope to minimum |
| **First community doesn't engage** | Medium | 🟡 Delays signal | Founder manually seeds rides; choose most active group |
| **Mobile UX issues** | Medium | 🟡 Frustrates users | Mobile test pass before Wave 1 |
| **Real-time messaging fails** | Low | 🟡 Coordination breaks | Fallback to WhatsApp (share numbers) |
| **Supabase RLS blocks legitimate queries** | Medium | 🟡 Users see empty data | Schema health check catches this; test with real user accounts |
| **Users don't understand "community"** | Low | 🟡 Confusion | Use familiar language: "your group", "your community" |
| **Founder burns out on manual ops** | Medium | 🔴 Beta stalls | Limit to 15 min/day; don't try to be customer support |
| **No rides available when user searches** | High | 🔴 Users churn | Founder posts rides; encourage drivers first; show ride requests |
| **Landing page looks fake** | Medium | 🟡 Trust issue | Fix fake stats BEFORE sharing link |
| **Google Maps API costs spike** | Low | 🟡 Cost | Set billing alerts; Maps has generous free tier |
